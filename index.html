Energy-saving tips

Simple actions for your home, one day at a time.

ϟ

Switch off lights

Turn lights off when leaving a room. Use daylight whenever possible.

♟

Unplug chargers

Disconnect chargers and devices when charging is complete, if safe to do so.

❄

Use AC wisely

Try a comfortable setting around 24–26°C and keep doors and windows closed.

♢

Save hot water

Take only the water you need and fix dripping taps promptly.

▤

Choose LED bulbs

When replacing bulbs, consider efficient LED options.

☼

Use natural light

Open curtains during the day when privacy and weather allow.

Terms & Conditions

Please read how this student demo works.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. About EnergySaver

EnergySaver is a beginner-friendly demonstration website that helps you create personal reminders for everyday energy-saving habits. It is provided for learning and demonstration purposes.

2. Reminder notifications

Reminders are checked in your browser while the website is open. Notifications require browser permission and may depend on your device, browser settings, and operating system. Alerts are not guaranteed if the tab is closed, the device is asleep, or permissions are blocked.

3. Data and privacy

In this version, reminder entries and theme preferences are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. The website does not include an account system or a server database. Clearing browser data may remove your saved reminders. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

4. Energy figures and advice

The website counts reminders you mark as completed. It does not measure electricity, water use, costs, or carbon emissions. Tips are general suggestions and are not a promise of specific savings. Use appliances according to their manuals and follow relevant safety guidance.

5. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for choosing safe actions and suitable reminder times. Do not unplug, switch off, or adjust equipment if doing so could create a safety risk or interrupt essential equipment.

6. Availability and changes

This demo is provided “as is” without a guarantee that every feature will work on every device. Features and these terms may be updated as the project develops.

7. Contact

This student demo currently has no support or contact service. For a school submission, replace this line with your project team's approved contact details if required.

By using this demo, you acknowledge these terms.
